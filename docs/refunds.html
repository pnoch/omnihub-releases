Refunds

Last updated 14 November 2026

If OmniHub does not work for you, ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. Include the email address used at checkout and, if you have it, the order id.

How to ask

Open a request at Support. Payments are processed by a merchant of record; refunds are issued through the same payment method.

Licence keys

A licence key is checked offline, so a key that has already been delivered cannot be revoked. After the 14-day window, refunds are at our discretion. Nothing here limits rights you have under the consumer law that applies to you.

Chargebacks

Please contact us first — most problems are a configuration issue we can fix. A chargeback started without contact may lead to the associated licence being refused in future releases.
