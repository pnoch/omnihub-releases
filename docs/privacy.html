Privacy

Last updated 14 November 2026

OmniHub is a local-first application. Your library — the videos, courses, transcripts, notes and settings you save — stays on your device. There is no OmniHub account and no library server.

What the app stores on your device

  • The media files and metadata you save.
  • Notes, bookmarks, transcripts and playback progress.
  • Your settings, including any Ollama endpoint you configure.
  • Your licence key, so the app can work without a network.

None of this is uploaded by the app. Wi-Fi sync sends library data only to the desktop address you enter on your own network.

What we receive

  • Purchases. Payments are handled by a merchant of record. We receive the buyer email, order id and the fact of payment — never card details.
  • Crash reports. The Android app can send a crash report with the device model, OS version and a stack trace. It does not include your library, filenames or notes.
  • Support messages. Whatever you choose to write in a support request.

Optional AI features

Translation and generated subtitles can use an Ollama endpoint you run and configure. Requests go directly to that endpoint; we do not proxy them and do not receive their content.

Payments

The checkout is operated by a merchant of record, which is the seller of record for the transaction and handles sales tax and VAT. Their privacy policy applies to the payment step.

Your choices

Uninstalling the app removes its local data. To ask about purchase records, use the support link below; records required for tax and accounting may be kept for the period the law requires.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. Material changes will be noted on this page.
